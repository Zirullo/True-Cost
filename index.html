True Cost: what if your car told you exactly what each km costs?

Drivers think in money, not in litres. Yet no cockpit shows the real cost of mobility in real time. This project proves it is already possible using Stellantis Connected Services and live price data.

⛽ Live consumption

Cost per km = consumption × pump price. Consumption follows speed on a real fuel-map curve — hold the ↑ Accelerate / ↓ Brake pedals (or the arrow keys) and watch the € / km move.

● Live prices

Local Price is the price on the totem of the next station ahead, with the distance to it counting down — watch for it on the right of the road. Stations scatter ± 15 cents around the live local average (MIMIT open data, stations within 50 km). Last Refuel Price is what the fuel in the tank actually cost — and the trip cost is computed on that.

● Trip & tank

Distance, litres burned and total euro accumulate live; the fuel level bar drains with them. Press RESET — on the cluster or on the deck — to start a new trip from zero.
