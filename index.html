Star*Up · Stellantis · BEV

What if your BEV told you exactly what each kilometre costs?

The True Cost BEV view — real-time energy cost per km in euros, calculated on your home charging tariff. The nearest fast charger price is always visible for comparison.
Selected for Star*Up 2026.

Power Slider

Right of zero — motor draws energy, cost rises.
Left of zero — regenerative braking: the motor becomes a generator, energy flows back to the battery. Cost per km goes negative and SoC recovers.
The green zone on the slider marks the regen range.

Regen Mode
OFF — no recovery LOW 55% efficiency HIGH 72% efficiency

Sets how much of the braking energy is captured. HIGH enables one-pedal driving — lift off and the car decelerates, recovering up to 72% of kinetic energy back as charge. With OFF, dragging left simply coasts with no recovery.

Time Warp

Up to 20× speed. Watch SoC drain and costs accumulate over a full journey in seconds. Press Reset to save.

● Trip History

Last 6 trips — date, km, kWh consumed and energy cost. Oldest fade left. Press Reset Trip to save the current run.
In production: logged automatically by Connected Services.

● Energy Price Feed

Home Charging — your tariff, set once, drives all calculations.
Fast Charger — nearest DC fast charger (simulated Free2Move). Delta shows the real cost of charging away from home.
In production: via Connected Services + TomTom EV Charging API.
