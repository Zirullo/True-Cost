True Cost: what if your car told you exactly what each km costs?

Drivers think in money, not in litres. Yet no cockpit shows the real cost of mobility in real time. This project proves it is already possible using Stellantis Connected Services and live price data.

⛽ Live consumption

Cost per km = consumption × pump price. Consumption follows speed on a real fuel-map curve — drag the Throttle slider and watch the € / km move.

● Live prices

Local Price is fetched from stations within 50 km (MIMIT open data). Last Refuel Price is what the fuel in the tank actually cost — and the trip cost is computed on that.

● Trip & tank

Distance, litres burned and total euro accumulate live; the fuel level bar drains with them. Time Warp up to 20× compresses a whole journey into seconds.

● Trip history

Press RESET — on the cluster or on the deck — to close the current trip and log it. The last 6 trips are kept, shown when the vehicle is stopped.
